Step-by-Step Guide

How to set up your bot

Follow these steps after signing up. Most customers are live in under 15 minutes.

1
Register at jamiitek.com/chatbot/register
Create your account, choose a plan, and enter your business details. Your account is ready instantly.
2
Complete the Setup Wizard
Name your bot, describe your business, add your services and FAQs. Just 3 simple steps.
3
Connect WhatsApp — scan a QR
Open the Connect page and scan the QR code with WhatsApp. No Meta account, no Phone Number ID, no waiting for approval.
4
Add it to your website (optional)
Copy the one line of code from the "Add to Website" page and paste it into your site. A floating chat button appears instantly.

Go live and test
Send a WhatsApp message to your number, or open the chat on your website. Your bot replies within 2 seconds! 🎉
