Questions

Before you start.

Can I switch paths later?
Yes. Many customers start with the AI or the free Builder, then move to a template or a custom website as they grow. Your content and your name come with you.
Do I need my own domain name?
No. Every site starts with a free address like yourname.jamiitek.com. When you're ready, we register a .co.tz, .tz or .com for you and connect it.
How do I pay?
With M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money or a bank transfer (CRDB, NMB). Custom projects get a fixed price in the proposal before any work starts.
Can I add a WhatsApp chatbot?
Yes — JamiiBot answers your customers 24/7 in Swahili and English and works with any of the four paths. See how JamiiBot works.
I'm not good with computers. Which path is for me?
Choose a template or a custom website — we do the setup for you. Or just message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you for free.
